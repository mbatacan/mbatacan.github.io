Project Experience with Atfal Mafkoda

Atfal Mafkoda is an organization that works to find missing children in the Middle East. Our team built a face aging generative AI system and a facial recognition matching pipeline to help identify children who have been missing for years.

The project won the Audience Choice Award at BU Spark Demo Day 2023.
