BU Spark! Fall 2023 TPM Projects

For my last semester of undergrad, I got the chance to be a technical project manager for BU's CS506: Computational Tools for Data Science class. Compared to the Justice Media Co-lab class, these student projects had more emphasis on machine learning component of data science. I led two different projects managing a total of 9 student teams. The projects I led were looking into the Boston government's budget and spending on police overtime, along with looking at how BU Student athlete performance and academic performance impact each other.
